## Rules of this ledger
1. A strategy may only enter the design if it has a ledger ID (no invented mechanisms).
2. Primary sources outrank secondary; secondary numbers are marked and must be re-verified at deploy time.
3. Free-tier quotas (E11–E13) are *variables, not constants* — the design must degrade gracefully (fallback chains, quota budgeting), which is itself the lesson of E11.
4. Simulation results produced by `cascade/simulate.mjs` are labeled **synthetic replication** — they demonstrate mechanism, and their *shape* replicates E6/E7 findings; they are never presented as vendor benchmarks.
